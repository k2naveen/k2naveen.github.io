This is my pre-recorded session for **XDC 2026**, introducing the Wayland
content frame rate protocol: a way for clients to tell the compositor the
native cadence of the content they are presenting, so the display pipeline can
be driven at a rate that actually matches it instead of forcing everything to
the output's fixed refresh rate.
